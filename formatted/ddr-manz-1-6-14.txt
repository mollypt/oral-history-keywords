JA: What was the best moment you ever had at camp?
AM: Well, when I graduated high school and then started to work full-time under my father, and then one year later the class of '45 had their graduation, and that's when I met my wife. [Laughs] And so I took a picture of the banquet and I made sure she was in the foreground in the picture. Then I guess that kind of started things going for me. [Laughs]
JA: And it turned out pretty good.
AM: Yeah.
JA: How much later did you, did you get married?
AM: It was not until 1949.
JA: That was a long courtship.
AM: Yeah.
JA: What was the worst moment you ever had at camp?
AM: You know, it's a funny thing, I've never taken a bath in a public bath, okay, but in camp we all had to take a shower in the same place with a lot of other people, and I just couldn't get myself to go take a shower in that place where there's other people. I guess I was too shy or something. So I would try to pick a time when I'd be the only one. Eventually I got used to it so it was okay, but that was one of the most difficult things for me to get over. But I got over it.
