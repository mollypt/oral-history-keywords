Jim was the original 110 percent American. And from the viewpoint of today, you might say that he was an unquestioning patriotic American. Anything the government did was to be supported. But that was the sort of attitude of most of the people of that era. And it wasn't until much, much later that people began to question the government, question what the government was doing.
