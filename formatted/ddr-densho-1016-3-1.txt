AD: How did you respond when you first started talking with your parents about it?
PC: They didn't talk about it a lot, and they didn't make a very big deal about it, so it didn't really register as having been as big as it was. And so I don't think I asked very more questions. PC: I don't know, I've thought about that over the years. And I think, I kind of think that they didn't talk about a lot of those things because of the betrayal I think maybe they felt that happened to them as citizens during the war. And plus, growing up in a household with ten people, there's a lot going on all the time when they had their own business farming and everything, and I think there was just so much going on that there wasn't really a time to sit down and talk about things with them.
