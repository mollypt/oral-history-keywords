And he'd tell me about the orphans in the train stations, and he felt so sorry about the orphans that he would get candy bars and things and give it to the children. And he'd always write and tell me all about Japan, how he'd go to, met some people and they'd invite him out forsukiyakidinner, and he'd say, "Oh, thesukiyakiin Japan was delicious." And I was just about to do that, and I was even considering going to Japan myself, because I was quite worried. But then I got a letter that said he contracted lockjaw, and he was in the hospital but he was okay.
