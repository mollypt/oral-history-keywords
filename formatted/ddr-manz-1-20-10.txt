Today, I am supposed to be talking about Manzanar, not Rohwer, and I remember that while these ladies were always well-meaning and nice, they were certainly hell-bent on making us Americans! At my age, I was growing up in a household where I spoke Japanese, and we had Japanese names, and my name Takayo is a beautiful name, it means "child of high ideals" and then these ladies were intent on "Americanizing" us, and I believe they were well-meaning.
