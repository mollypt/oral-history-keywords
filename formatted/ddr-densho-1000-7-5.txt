And he says, "No, I don't want you to do that. Unless... if you don't go to America, go to America and meet my son, he will never come back to Japan." Because my mother was pretty strict and she had desire to come back to Japan, which maybe my father had no desire. He was pretty talented. He used to play theshamisenand all these things and he used to teach -- I know when I was there, he used to teach these girls around our neighborhood. Japanese girls around the neighborhood come to the home and he'd teach 'em how to do Japanese dance, and I thought, "My dad is crazy," I said, "doing all this stuff," but that's what Mother said, "That's how he is."
