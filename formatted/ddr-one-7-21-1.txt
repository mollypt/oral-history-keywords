[This transcript is a translation of the original Japanese text.]
BB: Good morning.
MO: Good morning.
BB: Were you born in Portland?
MO: Yes.
BB: Yes. When were you born in Portland?
MO: I was born on July 28th in 1916.
BB: In 1916. Did you live in Portland for a while?
MO: That's what I was told, but I don't remember very well. [Laughs]
BB: Oh, I see. [Laughs] How many years did you live in Portland?
MO: I think it was just for two or three years. My parents took me back to Japan in '20, in 1920. That's all I know, and I don't remember anything about our life while we were here.
BB: Did you speak only Japanese with your family in those days when you were small?
MO: No, my parents told me that they managed a hotel. We were speaking more English than Japanese.
BB: Oh.
MO: I went back to Japan when I was around three and half years old. It was difficult for me to communicate in Japanese. My parents had a hard time trying to help me adjust. It didn't take me a long time to get used to the language; I was only three. I was fine after that. I forgot all my English though.
BB: Did you still speak English well when you were young?
MO: No, I lost my English very quickly. [Laughs]
BB: Where did you live in Japan? Who did you live with?
MO: I lived in Okayama prefecture with my grandfather and grandmother. I lived with my grandparents. My grandfather passed away when I was seven, well, in Japanese years, actually I was five or six. My grandmother was alive until I was in the third grade in high school. I was twelve or thirteen. My uncle took me under his wing, and that is where I grew up after that.
BB: What did your grandfather and grandmother do for a living?
MO: They were farmers. They were land owners and farmers. I was always helping them.
BB: Did you often see your mother and father while you lived in Japan?
MO: No. My father came to Japan on business when I was in the first grade in my girls' school. What year was that? I was twelve or thirteen. That was the only occasion. I didn't see them until 1955. I didn't see them until 1937.
BB: So you were educated in Japan.
MO: Yes.
BB: You lived there.
MO: I went to school only in Japan.
