And we get the galley copies and we'd translate that. We had a bunch of (Japanese) translators (and we'd check the) language. And then there's another group that took it over and they worked it into a brief for the (GHQ) staff. So that they'd have a copy of the translations of the morning papers when the papers went out locally. And among the translators, we had one guy named Higashi (who) was a CanadianNiseifrom Vancouver (that) was the first Japanese reporter to cover the Korean War.
