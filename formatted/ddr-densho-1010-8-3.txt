So I said, "What are you gonna do with your contraband?" And one of our advertising agents from theFresno Bee, he was in the U.S. Navy Intelligence. So he said, "Don't worry about it," he says, "I'll tell you when to turn it in. Because I worked in Intelligence and I know what's going on." Called the wholesaler, he says, "What do you got?" He says, "We got thirty cases of ammunition." Says, "Oh, wow, we can use that. There's going to be a shortage," so they took it all back.
