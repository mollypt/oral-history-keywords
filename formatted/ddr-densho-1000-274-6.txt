So we put the thermometer right there, and when the nurse come, we put in our mouth, go like that. "Hey, you got a high fever." "Yeah, I don't feel good." So they sent me to that, sent me home. Sent me home, so hey, we go to work in the cannery for thirty cents an hour. Thirty cents, we worked ten hours, three buck.
