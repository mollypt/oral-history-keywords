We did try to teach our children some Japanese culture, but I don't think we were very successful, particularly in the way of speaking and reading Japanese. We tried to teach them Japanese when they were growing up in grade school, I even hired a Japanese student from University of Penn to come up to our house every Saturday morning to teach them with basic Japanese textbooks and so forth.
