AL: What are your early memories of Rohwer, of life at Rohwer? Anything that you saw, smelled, did? You remember your apartment?
TO: Apartment? One room, one room barracks. I believe that we were in Block 12, and we were close to a forest, which was actually a swamp area.
AL: Was your family in their own apartment? Did you have strangers with you?
TO: We had a barrack, a barrack, one room.
AL: One room, but was there another family with you, or just the five of your family?
TO: In our barrack, just our family.
AL: Just your family?
TO: But this barrack was divided, so there's another family.
AL: Right, so if it's like Manzanar, the barracks buildings are 20 x 100, and then within that you have, they called them apartments, but they're essentially rooms that were about 20 x 25, so you were, you were probably in one of those rooms, one family.
TO: I remember little things, playing, playing in the forest and catching bees and I do remember that we had, the mess hall, there would always be snakes in jars. At times, they allowed some of the men to go out and rivers, and sometimes they could catch turtles. They would also bring back snakes, water moccasins and stick 'em in jars, and when we'd go into the messhall to eat, and you'd see all these creatures, it was kind of scary.
AL: And were the snakes still alive?
TO: Yeah... well, I don't know. [Laughs] I don't know, yeah. I remember the latrine, and the showers, and some of my friends, my father was a block manager, so he was a block manager in Block 12.
