Regarding interrogation of prisoners, the only experience that the United States could look for was an experience of the British army in their different wars with different people in Europe and throughout the world, and the British theory of interrogation was to be very positive, to use strong armed tactics and procedures in interrogation. And this type of interrogation was taught to our students reporting to the Solomon's Guadalcanal Campaign, and they found that if they used positive, strong armed tactics, the students, the POIs would just clam up and some of them would bite their tongue and commit suicide on the spot.
