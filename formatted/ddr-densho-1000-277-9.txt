RO: Well, Skip Enders, who worked in judiciary for Rodino called me. I was in the mayor's office and he said, "Rose, the bill has gone to the White House." And that was kind of a very special moment for all of us, so many, so many people who worked on this redress effort. And I was just happy to be a part, and to be in that room. And while I, we were standing and cheering as he was signing, and he goes into his remarks, he mentions my name, "Rose Ochi sent me this article."
