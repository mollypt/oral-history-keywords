And later, people were telling me that since we had property there, that they were saying, "Hey, you guys could, you boys could go back there and claim it." And I said, "No, not in our case. My dad made sure everything was left to my sister." So my dad was very organized that way. He made sure that things he told us, it was gonna be that way.
