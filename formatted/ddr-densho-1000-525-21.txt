MM: Well, you know, my parents, I think, again, not unlike many immigrant parents, their children were their life, or lives. And I think... you know, in retrospect, I'm sure it's been very difficult. Because while on the one hand, I mean, I recognize things like the fact that they worked their asses off. They worked so hard to do better for themselves and their kids. But also I didn't think enough about maybe the insecurities they had about me learning things that they had no idea about, they didn't understand. How to navigate various systems of government or transportation, all these kind of things, how to make a reservation at a restaurant, a lot of mundane things.
