AL: Do you remember any special teachers, good or bad?

She was, she got us started on field trips on Saturday. On Saturday, we, I guess we had permission to go, signed by our parents, and we would go to Eagle Rock. It was, we have a community called Eagle Rock, which is just beyond Highland Park. Eagle Rock could be seen by riding on the 210 freeway going east from L.A., and you see eagle.
