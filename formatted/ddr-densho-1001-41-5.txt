JD: What was it like for you, being only one of fourteen Japanese Americans in Merrill's Marauders?
GH: Well, there was, I believe there was over two hundred volunteers, and to be selected was a privilege.
JD: What was the basis for choosing you?
GH: Well, I think physical fitness, ruggedness, and command of the language.
JD: General Merrill has been quoted as saying, "As for theNisei, I couldn't have gotten along without them." How were you and the otherNiseitreated?
GH: Yes, we were treated well. But I do recall going over on the ship to India, the most frequent question that was asked of us, "What do you think the Japanese will do to you if they capture you?" We had a response by saying we didn't know what they had in mind, but they would have to run like hell to catch us. But, to be more serious, I did leave the States with four hand grenades. They were heavy so I eventually ended up with one. But that was for myself.
JD: So, for yourself, you believed that if you were captured by the Japanese you would be badly treated or killed?
GH: Yeah, yeah, yeah.
JD: I've read somewhere that General Merrill ordered his troops to protect you and the otherNiseiwith their lives.
GH: I heard that too. Yeah.
JD: Because you were so important to their mission.
GH: Well, I think there was two... I think we were important. At the same time, for instance, when we traveled or made any move, I did have an escort so that they would not mistake me for the enemy.
JD: What were the conditions like, out in the field, when you were in Burma? Translating documents or intercepts, what was that work like?
GH: Well, we did intercept message. In that respect I think Roy was very active. We did translate and we did carry out a lot of interrogations. I think we did our duty well.
JD: I think everybody would agree with that...
GH: It was teamwork. Not only translating, but the mule skinner who pulled the mule, pulled the mule. That was the only ground transportation we had. And your rifleman, your machine gunner, we all worked, we all took the war on our shoulders and we all performed well.
