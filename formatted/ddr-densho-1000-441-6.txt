Oh, I did have one teacher who told me her family had experienced World War I, she was a German. So then we went to the assembly and heard Roosevelt's speech, and I remember I was sitting toward the back because I was in sixth grade and we went through sixth grade. And so I was sitting in the back and listening, and I know that I was feeling uncomfortable. This connection to Japan, that was totally new to me, and obviously I wasn't happy about it. [Laughs] So then in the class, the class I was fine, and a new kid called me a name, and one of the regulars, they had a scuffle, that kind of thing.
