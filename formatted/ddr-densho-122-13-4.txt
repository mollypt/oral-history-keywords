But I didn't show any emotion because I believed what my husband and those boys were doing because I just, my brother was in the army, he was drafted and he went, and he said there's no chance because in the army, when the top people came to visit their place there, they were put away so that they couldn't see the -- I think the president or somebody came to their place.
