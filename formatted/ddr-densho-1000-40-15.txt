TI: Well, when you arrived, first arrived, at Camp Shelby, already there were the mainlander Japanese Americans. How did the two groups get along?
SK: Well, as I recall -- with me, I take a person as he is, not in general, so I felt I got along well with the mainlandNiseis, but there were some who... what you call it? Didn't get along, I guess, with them. Mainly because most of them were noncoms already.
TI: The mainland Japanese AmericansNiseis?
SK: Yeah, mainlandNiseis, and they gave the orders. And us Hawaiians don't like to take orders from another Japanese -- [laughs] -- so that was my feeling anyway. But I think I got along well with everybody because I got along with -- I mean, I respected a guy as he is right there and then. So that was all right for me.
