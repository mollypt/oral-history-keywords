AL: This is Alisa Lynch, this is part two of an interview with Jack Kunitomi, side B of tape 1. And you were talking about the wholesale markets. So how much did you make at the market?
YK: Wholesale market was better because it was not wholly Japanese-owned. You had all the different races, Chinese owner, Jewish, Italian, and they employed regardless of what you looked like because now the teamsters were active in the wholesale markets. And they were fairly strict on the hiring, strictly maybe scabs, employees. So they were, they were getting twenty-five, thirty-five, forty dollars depending on what kinds of wholesale work you were doing.

So we had fruits and pumpkins, watermelons, oh, we had all these goodies on the, my father's truck. I would help him, and eat fruits on the way, drink the soda, and unload.
