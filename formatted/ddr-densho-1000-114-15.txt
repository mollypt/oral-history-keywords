REM: And you get involved in the restarting of the Puyallup Valley Japanese American Citizens League. Could you explain how this starts again?
RTM: Well, it's, I can't remember what year that was -- 1947 probably or somewhere in that area. 1947, '48. Anyway, we reactivated the Puyallup Valley Chapter of JACL. As I mentioned earlier, there was a Tacoma chapter and a Valley chapter, and so there was not enough people that came back to Tacoma to form a JACL chapter, so we decided that we'd combine the two and call it the Puyallup Valley Chapter. And so members of that lived in Tacoma also joined that group. And Kaz Yamane, who was one of the leadingNiseisof that time, was the president, I think, that year. And so we reactivated Puyallup Valley Chapter.
