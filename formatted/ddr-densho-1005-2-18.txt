DT: And now California. And at the ferry building in San Francisco, met my friends, Dr. Albert Fiske, professor of San Francisco State College, and Caleb Foote. Friends met us and then drove us down to Japantown, Pole Street, down toward our home in, on Buchanan Street.
And there we are, parked in front of our house. We were fortunate enough to have a house to come back to. So there we are opening the door for the first time after three years in the desert. And then we found the bowl of roses there waiting for us by a fellow named Rainey Borde who left a bowl of roses for us.
