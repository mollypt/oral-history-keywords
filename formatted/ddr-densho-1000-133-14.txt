And you know, if you want to be honest, I think (Mike) may have been thinking, "Jesus, you know, just got our first continental Japanese American into Congress. This is Norm. He's my brother-in-law. I told Min, when he first talked to me, I think '79 or so he came to New York (to) my office at Random House. I said, "You're an old man. Don't do this." And he said, "No, I have to do it."
