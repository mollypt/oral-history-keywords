Unless something good came in on the commissary and you'd buy that on your own. Or the, you bring some leftover from the kitchen. Oh, and the other things that were really funny, I'll tell you, but I don't know if it was funny but it was kind of tragic. When you first went to camp, we went to the mess hall. And they issued these aluminum plates with, or these darned handles that, you know, if you didn't put it in there properly, and most people did not, it'd tip over and all your food goes out. We had more accidents on that. That was an army issue. I remember that, thinking, "Gee this is ridiculous." And they'd just pile everything on top of it.
