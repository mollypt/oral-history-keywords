NA: We had our annual picnic, and I'm quite sure it was held in my boss's pasture, my dad's boss had cows...

And so that was one, and then they had the, do you know what thekenjinkaiis? Okay, where each, where the families got together according to where they came from, from Japan, and so they had akenjinkaiand we used to also go and have soft drinks for the kids, and then beer andsakefor the parents. And just to socialize, we never had anything. But we had our own Japanese American track... what do you call it? Track meet, and that was at a particular city, and then all the Japanese families 50 miles radius from that central place, we used to have a sports contest.

NA: The older brothers used to sponsor it.
