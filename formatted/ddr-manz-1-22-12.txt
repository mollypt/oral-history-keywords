AL: What do you remember of the day that you left Los Angeles?
YK: Well, I know we had to pack. What did we pack? Beforehand, we had gone to an outdoor store, which was on Main Street, to buy what they suggested, knee-high boots because of rattlesnakes. [Laughs] Heavy socks, heavy jackets, things like that. But we didn't know that the army, the government would give us some, some peacoats, clothing. But we didn't buy too many clothes, because I think we had enough clothes for sand and desert.
AL: Did your family evacuate before Mas's or after?
YK: After.
AL: Okay, so her family was already gone?
YK: Yes. Her family went to Santa Anita. And because the first brother had a retail market, he took a little truck. And neighbors' belongings, the whole neighborhood, loaded his truck with clothing mostly, I guess. So I don't know what they did with the truck, they drove it in and probably somebody sold it for them.
AL: Did, what did your family do with your possessions?
YK: Well, I guess we just left it, yes. One thing I regret leaving was all the Glenn Miller records, Benny Goodman's records. Yes, those were my passion.
AL: Was that the most difficult thing to leave behind?
YK: [Nods] Because I had collected so many, because my situation, you know, every penny counted even though they were only thirty-five cents for these 78 records. Yes, because we, our hobby at that time was going to see Al Jarvis' record studio. He had a studio in Hollywood, and we used to go there on Saturdays, try out the records in a private booth, see if we liked those music or not. So that was hard to take, but in an emergency, it was an emergency, so...
AL: How did you get to the train station that day?
YK: I think somebody gave us a ride. The train station was on Fourth, just south of... what street? Yes, because I remember boarding, all the blinds drawn on the passenger side. MPs guarding it, helping with the ladies.
AL: How many MPs were there?
YK: Well, there were probably two to each car, passenger cars.
AL: Were they armed?
YK: Oh, yes, ammo belts.
AL: What else?
YK: Oh, I'm sure they had their rifles.
AL: Is that the first time you had been out of Los Angeles?
YK: No, no.
