RP: What was your diet like? Were there obviously a lot of vegetables?
SK: Yes, what they callokazu. Yeah, that was like meat and vegetables, very small slices of meat with a lot of vegetables and soy sauce and sugar. I think it's kind of like the teriyaki sauce. Or now what they call it would be pan fry, wok, you know, the Chinese style vegetable cooking. There was a lot of that, because while I don't think they could afford to buy meat, meat was pretty expensive, but vegetable was plentiful, easy to get. We did eat a lot of vegetables.
RP: And rice?
SK: Yes, rice was a staple. There was rice with every meal except breakfast. I mean, they did cook fried rice when there was an abundance of rice leftover.
