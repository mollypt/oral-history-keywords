JD: What was it like being in the Military Intelligence Service Language School?
GH: Well, of course being in the military intelligence, I mean, that itself indicated I knew what I would be doing in general, but I didn't know anything in specific. Well, we did, from the courses we had, you could anticipate listening to radio. We also translated captured documents. And we did have a course on interrogation. So I did have a general idea what I would be doing.
JD: So I've seen from, the kind of work that you would be doing, this intelligence work, the army must have had the very highest level confidence and trust in you.
GH: I know. They did a thorough investigation. In my case I did apply for the FBI at one time, and when I was interrogated, a man came in and interviewed... walked in with a file that thick. [Laughs] So I know they did a thorough check.
JD: Well it seems... I hadn't thought of this before, but it seems as if maybe the opportunity to be in the military intelligence would have given you the best chance you could have to prove what you needed to prove. That you were the...
GH: I think so. Perhaps that was the field I was best qualified to do, yes.
JD: When did theNiseifirst hear about the 5307 Composite Unit?
GH: Well, actually, when we volunteered and we assembled in San Francisco, or Angel Island, we went by code name GALAHAD. And the 5307th came in, I heard about it while we were training in India.
JD: When you went to India first, what did they say you were training for?
GH: For long range penetration and jungle training. So we, by then, we knew that we were going into Burma.
JD: When you volunteered for the 5307, what did they tell you your job was going to be? Did they get specific about it?
GH: No. All I heard was that it was a dangerous and hazardous mission. And I subsequently learned that by War Department estimate, they had anticipated 85 percent casualty.
JD: But they didn't tell you that right away?
GH: Well, others had heard, but I didn't.
JD: Or maybe you heard about it, but you just decided to just forget about it.
GH: [Laughs] Maybe. It's been a while.
