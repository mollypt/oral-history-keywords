And they, a longshore leader named Phil Lily had the idea of writing this book, so he applied for a grant, and they gave it to him. It was really sort of unheralded. But he had this idea that, "Hey, we can tell the story of Tacoma through this union, with all its aspects." And so they started in, they hired a lady who they didn't like her writing, and so they fired her and the Humanities Commission man asked me to go and help, because I'd done a lot of humanities projects. And so I went over and they, they talked to me and a good friend of mine at the Pacific Lutheran University, the two of us wrote the book calledThe Working Waterfront.
