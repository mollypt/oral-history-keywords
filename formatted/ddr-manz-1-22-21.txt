AL: So were you drafted?
YK: Yes.
AL: Do you remember when? It was '43 or '44?
YK: '43... gosh.
AL: That's okay, I was just curious. So where did you, when you were drafted, did you know what branch of the service you would go to?
YK: Not really, because we were, most of us were sent to Denver. Denver had a big army post where we took our physical. I think then after physical, we were placed on inactive reserve, so we were sent back to Heart Mountain. So that, that was to be in camp for a month or two. So I guess that was the early, early April when we were in Denver. In the meantime, the young people were being drafted left and right, they were sent to all the different training sites. Finally in July, July 6th, I think it was, we were called to Denver to be enrolled.
AL: Did you have, when you went back to Heart Mountain after you were drafted, did you have anybody say anything to you or question your decision, like any of the Fair Play guys? Was there any interaction?
YK: No.
