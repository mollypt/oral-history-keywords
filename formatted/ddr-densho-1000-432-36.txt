I thought we might be able to grow lettuce in Japan. I talked about it to an occupation army officer I was working with. I went to talk to people at Japan Agricultural Cooperatives in Tokyo. They agreed to plant the new vegetables in Japan. We conducted tests in their greenhouse. It was very successful. Then they tested in Nagano and then Chiba. People were very pleased. I started the project but the official record says the occupation army introduced it to Japan.
